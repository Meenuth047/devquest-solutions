### 🔍 Daily SQL: High-Value Top Spenders

We have tables `customers` (`id`, `name`, `country`) and `orders` (`id`, `customer_id`, `amount`, `order_date`).

Write a query to find each customer's `name` and their total order amount as `total_spent` for all customers who spent strictly more than $100.
Order by `total_spent` descending.

---

## Verified Solution (sql)

```sql
-- Find customers who spent > 100 ordered by total_spent DESC:
SELECT c.name, SUM(o.amount) AS total_spent
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o ON c.id = o.customer_id
GROUP BY c.id, c.name
HAVING total_spent > 100
ORDER BY total_spent DESC;
```
