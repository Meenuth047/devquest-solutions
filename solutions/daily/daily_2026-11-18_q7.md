### ⚡ Daily Drill: Hashtag Extractor (Regex)

Write a regular expression pattern that extracts all hashtag identifiers from social media text.
A hashtag starts with `#` followed by one or more alphanumeric characters or underscores (`[a-zA-Z0-9_]`).

**Capture Group (1):** Extract the tag name without the leading `#` symbol.

**Example:** `'Loving #python and #coding_life on #day100!'` ➔ `['python', 'coding_life', 'day100']`

---

## Verified Solution (regex)

```regex
PATTERN = r"#([a-zA-Z0-9_]+)"
```
