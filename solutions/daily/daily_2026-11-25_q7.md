### ⚡ Daily Drill: Domain Extractor (Regex)

Write a regular expression that extracts the domain name (including TLD) from a URL string.
For example: from `https://sub.github.com/path` or `http://google.com?q=1`, extract `sub.github.com` or `google.com`.

**Capture Group (1):** The domain string.
