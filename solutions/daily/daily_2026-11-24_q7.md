### ⚡ Daily Drill: ISO Date Extractor (Regex)

Write a regular expression pattern that extracts all ISO 8601 calendar dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.

**Format:** Exactly 4 digits, a hyphen, exactly 2 digits, a hyphen, and exactly 2 digits, on word boundaries.

**Example:** `'Events on 2026-01-15 and 2026-12-31 recorded.'` ➔ `['2026-01-15', '2026-12-31']`

---

## Verified Solution (regex)

```regex
PATTERN = r"\b(\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2})\b"
```
